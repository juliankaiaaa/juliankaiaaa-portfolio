CapsiCAM

My role Research and Data Coordinator Group project

An Android app developed for our undergraduate thesis to classify chili pepper leaf images as Healthy, Bacterial Leaf Spot, or Cercospora Leaf Spot. It runs predictions on the device, displays visual heatmaps, and lets users save and revisit their scans.
